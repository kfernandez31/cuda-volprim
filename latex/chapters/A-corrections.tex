\chapter{Reference corrections: patch and verification record}
\label{app:corrections}

This appendix documents the corrections to the reference's volumetric-primitive
integrators that \Cref{sec:results-firefly} diagnoses and that every corrected-reference
measurement in this thesis uses. They were submitted upstream as a pull
request~\cite{KramarzVolprimFixes2026}; because the upstream repository is private, the
record here---the finding, each change, and the verification---is kept self-contained.
The patch comprises two commits touching two files ($+32/-8$ diff lines: $+30/-6$ in the
five-fix commit, $+2/-2$ in the mixture-kernel companion).

\section{The mirrored integral, restated}
\label{app:corrections-claim}

In the finite-bounds branch of \texttt{density\_integral}, the quantity $B$ is computed from
$p = \texttt{ray.o} - \texttt{center}$. The implemented erf combination
\[
e^{-\frac{1}{2}(C-B^2)}\,\sqrt{\tfrac{\pi}{2}}\;
\Big[\operatorname{erf}\!\Big(\tfrac{B}{\sqrt{2}}\Big) -
     \operatorname{erf}\!\Big(\tfrac{B-T}{\sqrt{2}}\Big)\Big]
\]
is, however, the antiderivative for the opposite sign convention ($B$ built from
$\texttt{center} - \texttt{ray.o}$). The correct segment integral (whitened coordinates,
$\lVert w\rVert = 1$, segment $[0,T]$, $B = w\cdot p$, $C = p\cdot p$) is
\[
\tau(0,T) \;=\; e^{-\frac{1}{2}(C-B^2)}\,\sqrt{\tfrac{\pi}{2}}\;
\Big[\operatorname{erf}\!\Big(\tfrac{T+B}{\sqrt{2}}\Big) -
     \operatorname{erf}\!\Big(\tfrac{B}{\sqrt{2}}\Big)\Big].
\]
The implemented expression equals it under $t \to -t$: it is the integral over $[-T,0]$---the
segment mirrored about its start. The two coincide only when the reflection maps the
one-dimensional Gaussian onto itself, i.e.\ when the segment \emph{starts} exactly at the
ray's closest approach to the centre ($B = 0$), and in the two-sided full-line limit. (A
segment \emph{centred} on the peak, $B=-T/2$, is instead maximally mis-integrated.) The
full-range branch is therefore unaffected, which is why whole-primitive queries and tests
built on them pass. Because scatter vertices concentrate near density peaks, marched
segments tend to start near $B\approx 0$, exactly where the error vanishes. The error thus
stays small in typical renders while remaining large for asymmetric segments (the probe's
\num{5.85}$\times$ additivity discrepancy). The corrected window
restores the asymmetric-bounds two-erf form given in the reference's arXiv~v2 preprint (its
Eq.~14; the ACM version's Eq.~14 is the symmetric-bounds single-erf special case)~\cite{DSYG}. The four
accompanying corrections (shadow-march segment, support truncation, depth-0 MIS weight, phantom
re-entry) are described in \Cref{sec:results-firefly}.

\section{The patch}
\label{app:corrections-patch}

The two commits (\texttt{7171e1f8}, \texttt{1681538b}) touch \texttt{common.py} and
\texttt{volprim\_prb.py}, both under \texttt{volprim/integrators/}. The
byte-exact diff is in the pull request~\cite{KramarzVolprimFixes2026} and in the digital
supplement accompanying this thesis. The five changes:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Segment-integral window} (\texttt{common.py}, \texttt{GaussianKernel} segment
  branch; the same two lines again in \texttt{GaussianGaborMixtureKernel}, second commit).
  The erf endpoints are re-paired to the convention the code's $B$ actually uses:
  \begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
  before:~~arg\_plus~~= sqrt\_half * B\\
  ~~~~~~~~~arg\_minus~= sqrt\_half * (t\_limit - B)\\[3pt]
  after:~~~arg\_plus~~= sqrt\_half * (t\_limit + B)\\
  ~~~~~~~~~arg\_minus~= -sqrt\_half * B
  \end{quote}
  \item \textbf{Shadow-march segment} (\texttt{volprim\_prb.py}, march loop): the marched
  primitive's integral bounds change from the unbounded full-range branch
  (\texttt{tmin=None, tmax=None}) to the forward segment from the entry,
  \texttt{tmin=si.t}, resolving the integrator's own \texttt{TODO}.
  \item \textbf{Support truncation} (both \texttt{eval\_transmittance} sites): the
  integration end becomes $\min(\texttt{ray.maxt}, t_{\text{support exit}})$, the exit
  distance obtained from the existing \texttt{ray\_ellipsoid\_intersection} helper---so
  shadow rays see the same extent-truncated medium the flight sampler simulates.
  \item \textbf{Depth-0 escape weight} (\texttt{volprim\_prb.py}): the environment sampling density (pdf) is forced to zero at depth $0$ with an explicit per-lane \texttt{dr.select}, restoring MIS
  weight $1$ for camera rays that reach the background directly. (The original passed the
  depth condition through \texttt{pdf\_direction}'s \texttt{active} argument, which is a
  lane hint, not a value gate.)
  \item \textbf{Phantom re-entry guard} (\texttt{common.py}, \texttt{primitive\_tracing};
  one-step variant in the march): before pushing a newly entered primitive onto the overlap
  stack, the stack is scanned for that primitive's identity, and a duplicate push is
  rejected. This cannot discard a real event: a straight ray crosses a convex boundary at most
  twice---one entry, one exit---so a second entry of the same primitive is necessarily spurious.
  The scan mirrors the loop idiom the tracer already uses for exit-removal.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Deterministic self-checks}
\label{app:corrections-probes}

Two checks use only the reference's own kernel functions---no scene, no cross-renderer
comparison. Check (i) is \emph{additivity}, $\tau(a,b) + \tau(b,c) = \tau(a,c)$: three calls
to one function, insensitive to any normalisation convention. Check (ii) is the \emph{ratio}
of the kernel's segment integral to a brute-force numerical quadrature of its own
\texttt{eval}, which must be one constant wherever the segment lies. On the unpatched
upstream \texttt{main} (production mixture kernel, single unit Gaussian), the two halves of
a split segment sum to \num{5.85} times the whole. The ratio to quadrature drifts by a
factor of \num{2.7} across three adjacent segments. On the patched branch, additivity is
exact and the ratio is constant. Run against the \emph{released} revision's kernel
(\texttt{622fabd}), the same additivity check passes (factor \num{1.0000} on the
asymmetric splits): the mirrored window was introduced by the rewrite, not inherited from
the release. The probe scripts that produce these numbers accompany
the pull request and the digital supplement.

The reference's oscillatory kernel branch (\texttt{is\_gabor}: Gaussians modulated by a
cosine, for high-frequency detail) does not yet pass the additivity check after the window
correction, indicating at least one further discrepancy in its series-erf evaluation. It is
flagged upstream and out of scope here: no measurement in this thesis uses the oscillatory
path.

\section{Verification summary}
\label{app:corrections-verification}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Reconciliation of the reference's own revisions} (WDAS cloud, meadow
  environment, analog mode, same scene). The reference's published DSYG-release code renders
  \num{0.328}, and its current revision as shipped renders \num{0.289}. With only the
  two-line sign correction, the current revision renders \num{0.332}---back in line with the
  release. Each reference arm is a single \num{256}-spp render, so these are reconciliation
  levels, not converged statements; the corrected arm is firefly-noisy at this budget.
  This renderer (\num{0.321}, the converged pool of \Cref{sec:scattering-ladder}) is not
  required anywhere in that comparison.
  \item \textbf{Spaced-chain furnace} (albedo 1, uniform unit environment, ground truth
  exactly 1): worst configuration improves from $+4.2\,\%$ to $\le 0.02$ percentage points
  across $N{=}1$--$16$ primitives, spacings 1--4, $\sigma$ 1--60.55, and anisotropic rotations.
  The Newton solver and the LLVM backend are spot-checked on top of the bisection/CUDA
  defaults; analog rows are bit-exact $1.0$ throughout.
  \item \textbf{Exact-arithmetic check}: an 85-primitive extract of the cloud's dense core,
  absorption-only (deterministic), matches an exact float64 predictor (per-chord closed-form optical depth, summed in double
precision independently of the renderer) to 5 decimal places
  with the corrections; without them, thin chords are $+36$--$43\,\%$ too thick (the phantom
  re-entry).
  \item \textbf{Depth-0 background} (deterministic): an empty scene renders \num{0.99371}
  instead of \num{1.0} before the correction, exactly $1/(1+(4\pi)^{-2})$.
  \item \textbf{Cross-renderer certification}: after the corrections, this renderer and the
  reference's next-event estimator agree at the Monte-Carlo noise level
  (\Cref{fig:agreement-panels}), and both follow the exact $-\tfrac12$ convergence slope
  (\Cref{fig:nee-convergence}).
\end{itemize}

\noindent
\emph{Scope.} All verification is of primal rendering---the ordinary forward pass from scene
to image. The differentiable path (parameter gradients) is
structurally preserved (the edits sit inside the existing \texttt{resume\_grad} blocks) but
was not re-validated. The reference's documented shadow-march early-out
(\texttt{total\_tr} threshold) is a deliberate speed/accuracy trade-off of its author and is
left untouched. Its measured effect on the cloud is $-0.35\,\%$ full-image mean and up to
$-18\,\%$ in near-black regions.
