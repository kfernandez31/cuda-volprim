# MSc Thesis — Full Examiner Review (2026-06-16, session 00296071)

## Overall assessment

This is a strong, unusually honest thesis whose deliberately de-hyped stance is its defining methodological virtue, not a weakness to be corrected. Thirteen specialist agents examined every chapter, and every issue initially raised as Blocking was adversarially re-verified against the banked EXRs, CSVs and logs; **zero Blocking issues survived verification**. Every load-bearing quantitative claim reproduces from the banked artefacts: the +156% Mitsuba-NEE energy bias, the +0.4% MIS bias, the ~59× clipped equal-quality headline, the flat-env ~0.6× net (Mitsuba ahead), the ~1.48× RIS win, the absorption RMSE/median-zero agreement, the 578/838/1200 MiB VRAM figures, the N^0.40 square-grid scaling, and the per-asset caps all re-derive to within rounding. The headline verdict is therefore that **the thesis's central claims hold**, and where they currently overstate, they overstate *against* the author's own interest (the 59× is conservative; the flat-env result is a documented loss the author volunteers). The remaining work is entirely Should-fix and Polish: a cluster of precise-number provenance gaps (clip-method wording, an uncaptured Mitsuba flat-env render time, a silently-restricted scaling fit, an undisclosed VRAM polling asymmetry, one ~1e-5-vs-7e-4 single-Gaussian agreement claim that the figure's own annotation contradicts) and a small set of cross-chapter inconsistencies. None of these change a conclusion; all of them are fixable without adding a single hyped word, and several fixes (disclosing the uncaptured Mitsuba timing, the global-not-per-pixel clip) make the thesis *more* honest, which is exactly the register the author already operates in.
